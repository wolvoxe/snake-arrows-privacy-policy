Privacy Policy for Snake Arrows

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Cem Sengezer ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Snake Arrows mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App on iOS and Android platforms.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The App is designed to store your game progress, high scores, and local preferences directly on your device. However, to provide core functionalities such as advertisements and in-app purchases, certain data is automatically collected by integrated third-party services.

Automatically Collected Data

When you access the App, third-party services may automatically collect information, including:

  • Device Data: Mobile device ID, model, manufacturer, operating system version, and device language.
  • Advertising Data: Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or Google Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Usage Data: Application interactions, crash logs, and purchase history.

2. Third-Party Tools & Services

We use specific third-party tools to operate the App, display advertisements, and manage in-app purchases. These third parties have their own privacy policies addressing how they use your information.

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may use cookies and mobile advertising identifiers to serve ads based on your past visits to the App or other apps and websites.

  • Data Collected: IP address, advertising identifiers, device information, and interaction with ads.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt-out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings (e.g., enabling "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy & Terms

B. RevenueCat (In-App Purchases)

We use RevenueCat to manage and validate our in-app purchases (such as the "Remove Ads" feature). RevenueCat helps us understand subscription and purchase states without requiring us to process your payment details directly.

  • Data Collected: Anonymous App User IDs, purchase receipts, subscription status, and basic device metrics.
  • Privacy Policy: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. In-App Purchases and Payment Processing

All in-app purchases (including one-time purchases and subscriptions) are processed securely through the respective platform's app store (Apple App Store for iOS devices, and Google Play Store for Android devices). We do not collect, process, or have access to your raw payment information, credit card details, or billing address. Your payment information is subject to the privacy policies of Apple or Google.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by third-party services is used to:

  • Deliver targeted and non-targeted advertisements.
  • Process and validate in-app purchases and unlock premium features (e.g., removing ads).
  • Analyze app usage to fix crashes and improve overall game performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions such as the EU). If you become aware of any data we have collected from children under age 13, please contact us using the contact information provided below, and we will take reasonable steps to delete such information.

6. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information provided to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Effective Date" of this Privacy Policy. You are encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy to stay informed of updates.
